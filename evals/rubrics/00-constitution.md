# Rubric: Constitution

Criteria format: `- ID | weight | check`. Weight 1 to 3. Scores 0 to 2 are given by the judge.

- CON-01 | 3 | The mission is a single clear sentence that names who it serves and what it does.
- CON-02 | 3 | There are at least five MUST / MUST NOT principles and each is testable (a reviewer could say pass or fail).
- CON-03 | 2 | Every principle and technology choice states a reason (the "why").
- CON-04 | 2 | Technology constraints say which decisions are locked and which are open.
- CON-05 | 2 | Quality bar covers testing, security and performance with concrete expectations, not slogans.
- CON-06 | 2 | The out-of-scope list is explicit and justified.
- CON-07 | 3 | No two rules contradict each other, and nothing reads as an AI assumption the owner never stated.
