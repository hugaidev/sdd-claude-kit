# Rubric: Product Requirements Document

- PRD-01 | 3 | Every functional requirement has a testable Given/When/Then acceptance criterion with no vague words ("fast", "easy", "etc.").
- PRD-02 | 3 | Requirements describe WHAT and WHY only; no implementation details (frameworks, tables, endpoints, class names).
- PRD-03 | 2 | The problem statement explains who has the pain, the evidence, and the current workaround.
- PRD-04 | 2 | Goals are measurable and non-goals are explicit.
- PRD-05 | 2 | Requirements are prioritised (MoSCoW) and the order matches the stated goals.
- PRD-06 | 2 | Non-functional requirements include at least performance and one of security, accessibility or reliability, each with a measure.
- PRD-07 | 2 | No feature appears without a workflow that uses it, and every workflow has a happy path and key error paths.
- PRD-08 | 1 | Assumptions, risks and open questions are listed with an owner.
- PRD-09 | 3 | The PRD is consistent with the constitution (no requirement violates a principle).
